Privacy Policy — Last updated: June 2026

1. Introduction

Go Morocco ("we", "our", "the app") is a tourism guide application for Morocco. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

2. Data We Collect

  • Email address — only if you create an account or sign in with Google
  • User ID — a unique identifier generated by Firebase Authentication
  • Favourites — cities and places you save, stored locally on your device (localStorage)

We do not collect: location data, contacts, photos, payment information, or any other personal data.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate your account (login/logout)
  • To save your favourites across sessions

We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising purposes.

4. Guest Mode

You can use Go Morocco without creating an account by choosing "Continue without account". In this mode, no personal data is collected. Favourites are saved only on your device.

5. Firebase & Google Services

We use Firebase (by Google) for authentication. When you sign in with Google or email, your data is processed by Google according to their Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention

Your account data is retained as long as your account exists. You can request deletion of your account and data at any time by contacting us.

7. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Union, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
